# Running your club on MFTournament

A guide for club managers, from the invitation to your first teamsheet.
Everything here happens at **myfootballtournament.com**. It works on a phone.

---

## 1. Take over your club

The organiser sends you a link. Open it.

The page names the club you are about to run. Check the name is yours, then fill in:

- **Your name** — how the organiser sees you.
- **Email address** — this is your login. There is no username.
- **Password** — see the rules below.

Press **Take over [club name]** and you are in.

Two things to know:

- The link works **once** and expires after **two weeks**. If it has been used or has gone stale, ask the organiser for a new one.
- If the invitation was issued from a competition, taking it up also **enters your club into that competition**. The page tells you which one before you press the button.

Already have an account on the site? Sign in first, then open the link — the club is added to the account you are signed in with.

### Password rules

- At least **7 characters**
- Must contain **letters and digits** (for example `redlions7`)
- No other requirement — no symbols, no capitals, no expiry

### Signing in later

Go to **myfootballtournament.com/login** and use your email and password.
Forgotten it? **Forgot password** on that page sends a reset link to your email.

---

## 2. Your club page

After signing in you land on **My Club**. Everything below is on that one page:

- **In the table** and **Next match** — where you stand and who you play next
- **The club** — name, colours, crest, team photo
- **Competitions** — the ones you are in, and who is registered for each
- **Teamsheets** — who played in each match
- **Squad** — everyone at the club

---

## 3. Change the club name, colours and links

In **The club**, press **Edit**. You can change:

- **Name**
- **Colours** — one or two. They paint your club's public pages. Press **Second colour** to add one, **One colour only** to drop it.
- **Founded** — a date, optional
- **Instagram, Facebook, YouTube** — full addresses, optional
- **Show players' ages on the public pages** — a tick for the whole squad. Dates of birth are never published either way; the site works out the age.

Press **Save**. Nothing is saved until you do.

---

## 4. Upload the crest

In **The club**, press the crest square at the top left and choose an image.

- It saves on its own — there is no Save button for it.
- JPG, PNG, WebP or GIF. Up to 5 MB; the site shrinks it for you.
- A square image works best. The crest is shown small and round in tables.
- The site reads the badge's main colour from the file and uses it on your public club header, so upload the real badge rather than a screenshot with a background.

---

## 5. Upload the team photo

Lower down in **The club**, under **Team photo**, press the frame and choose an image.
It also saves on its own. Landscape works best — it is shown full width on your public club page.

---

## 6. Add players

Scroll to **Squad**. The row at the bottom is the quick add:

| Field | Required |
|---|---|
| No. (shirt number) | no |
| First name | yes, or a last name |
| Last name | yes, or a first name |
| Position | no |

Press **Add**. The player appears in the list at once. Repeat for the whole squad — it is the fastest way to get everyone in.

To correct a player, press the **pencil** on their row. To release them, press the **bin**. On a phone, tap the row to bring the two buttons out.

### The rest of a player's details are optional

Click a player's name to open their page. Nothing here is required — a squad of names and numbers is a perfectly complete squad. Fill in what you have:

- **Photograph** — press the frame, it saves on its own
- **Height (cm)**, **Weight (kg)**, **Stronger foot**
- **Date of birth** — never published; only the age is shown, and only if your club shows ages
- **Instagram, Facebook**
- **Show on the public pages** — untick to leave a player out of the squad visitors see

Press **Save** on this page when you are done. The photograph is the exception: it saves the moment you choose it.

---

## 7. Register your players for a competition

Being in a competition and being **registered to play in it** are two different things.

Under **Competitions**, each competition you are in has a button showing something like **7 of 14**. Press it. Tick the players who are registered for that competition, then press **Save squad**.

- **Everyone** ticks the whole squad. **Nobody** clears it.
- Some competitions are open: leave everyone ticked and anyone you sign later joins automatically.
- Some competitions register their squads strictly. There the list is exact: only ticked players can be named in a match, and a player you sign later must be added to this list before he plays. A competition like this shows **squad needed** in orange until you save a squad.
- If the organiser has closed squads, the ticks are locked and the row says so. Ask the organiser.

Do this once per competition. A player can be in one squad and out of another.

---

## 8. Enter another competition

Under **Competitions**, press **Join another competition**. Search by competition, season or organiser, and press **Apply** next to the one you want.

Your application goes to that organiser, who accepts or refuses it. A refused application can be sent again with **Ask again**. You are in once they accept — then register your squad as in section 7.

---

## 9. Name your teamsheets

Under **Teamsheets** you see your fixtures, soonest first, and then your results.

For each match, name who played for your club. This is the only record of appearances anywhere on the site: a player who is never named on a teamsheet has played nothing, however many matches he was at.

You can name a side before or after the match — there is no deadline. You can only name your own side; the opponent's is theirs.

---

## 10. What you cannot do

- **Enter results.** Scores, goals, assists and cards are the organiser's.
- **Delete the club.** It may sit in someone else's league.
- **Add or remove other managers.** Who runs a club is decided by the organiser's invitations.

Anything in that list, ask your organiser.

## Quick reference

| I want to… | Where |
|---|---|
| Sign in | myfootballtournament.com/login |
| Change club name or colours | My Club → The club → Edit → Save |
| Change the crest | My Club → The club → press the crest |
| Change the team photo | My Club → The club → Team photo |
| Add a player | My Club → Squad → bottom row → Add |
| Add a photo, height, date of birth | My Club → Squad → click the player |
| Register players for a competition | My Club → Competitions → the "x of y" button → Save squad |
| Enter a new competition | My Club → Competitions → Join another competition |
| Say who played | My Club → Teamsheets |
